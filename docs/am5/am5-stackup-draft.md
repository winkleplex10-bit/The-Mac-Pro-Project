# MacPro6,2 AM5 CPU board: 10-layer JLC stackup DRAFT
2026-10-21 (ET), updated 2026-10-23. **NON-FAB draft. Widths are ESTIMATES pending SI & Fab's check in JLC's impedance calculator.** Box only.

## Total thickness: DECIDED 1.6 mm (Aidan, 2026-10-23)
- **Stackup: JLC10161H-1080A**, 1.612 mm, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner. This supersedes the earlier 10161H-2116 / 10201H-2116 / 2313A shortlist.
- **Why 1.6 mm:** base board J1 is the Amphenol ME1022410103011 (Mini Cool Edge 224), rated for a 1.57 mm card. No 224-position 1.97 mm part is listed. Apple's 2.04 mm board has a full-thickness 2.0 mm tongue, so a thinned tongue isn't an option.
- **J1 stiffener:** a stiffener goes at the J1 card-edge tongue. Its design is with Mechanical & Thermal.
- **Via rules:** keep the 0.15 mm drill via rules. Aspect ratio is about 10.7:1 at 1.612 mm. SI & Fab to confirm against JLC's limit.
- **Gen5 PCIe pairs go on the lower stripline (L8).** Layer roles below were updated to match. Widths are still estimates until SI & Fab runs JLC's calculator for 1080A.
- **Screw stack:** Aidan reuses Apple's stock shoulder screws (12.72 mm CPU board to core, 12.78 mm backplate to contact frame). A 0.4 mm stainless or copper washer goes at the board face under each core screw (H1-H4) and each frame screw (H5-H8), so the stack matches Apple's 2.04 mm. Washer OD/ID waits on Aidan's head and shoulder measurements. Holes: H3/H4 are 5.5 mm (power-supply side), H1/H2 are 5.0 mm (card-edge side), and H2's 5.0 x 6.0 mm slot is pending Aidan's OK.
- KiCad still holds a placeholder stackup. Updating it to 1080A is a separate pass.

Observations (estimate): 85 Ω and 90 Ω diff fit only on options with thicker L2–L4 spacing (≥ ~0.2 mm); many 0.1 mm-core options force sub-0.09 mm widths at 90 Ω. DDR5 40-50 Ω SE is easy everywhere. Final pick follows thickness measurement.

## Public-source confirmation status
- **PCIe lane reversal / polarity inversion on Raphael/Granite Ridge: NOT confirmed by any public source → GAP.** Reserve a layer change (via pair + GND return vias) at the connector (CPU-LINK J1) end of every PCIe pair.
- **GPP_CLK0-5 REFCLK routing / which clock serves which port: NOT confirmed → GAP** (ball names from map only). Route as 85/100 Ω-flexible diff pairs on L3, keep a spare via pair.

## SI & Fab review items
1. USB/DP escape rows A-K go on inner stripline L3 under the VRM area, with **~5 mm keep-out from any switch node** (no SW-node copper over them on L1, GND L2/L4 unbroken).
2. VDDCR phases **top-right**, VDDCR_SOC phases **top-left**; **no VRM on the left edge** (PCIe only).
3. PCIe lanes 0-15 and 16-27 on separate inner layers (L3 vs L8) or, if on one layer, separated by a stitched GND via fence.
4. Room for a layer change at the connector end in case lane reversal stays unconfirmed.
5. **12 layers?** Likely needed if DDR5 2 channels + 28 PCIe lanes + USB/DP cannot escape the 0.81 × 0.94 mm hex pitch on two stripline layers; commodity AM5 boards are commonly 6-8 layers with more surface routing, but our tighter outline and full-plane rule make 10 tight. Recommendation: plan for 10, keep a 12-layer fallback (adds a third stripline + GND pair). Needs SI & Fab's breakout study.
