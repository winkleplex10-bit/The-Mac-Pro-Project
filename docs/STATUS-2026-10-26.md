# MacPro6,2 — Design Status (2026-10-26)

## Baseline spec (locked 2026-10-25, AM5 only)
- CPU: AM5 Ryzen 7000/9000.
- PCIe 5.0 x16 to GPU; PCIe 5.0 x8 to SSD board (bifurcated, 2–4 NVMe).
- 6× USB-C (2 USB4, 2 DP Alt, 2 USB 3.2), 4× USB-A 3.0, 2× 2.5GbE, 1× HDMI 2.1b.
- Open conflicts: current GPU links Gen4 x8 (slot built for Gen5 x16?); HDMI 2.1 needs an FRL retimer; USB4 video vs. 4 GPU display outputs.

## Firmware
- Route: licensed AMI/Insyde (IBV) BIOS; OpenCore preloaded on a soldered onboard NVMe (never on user drives). IBV outreach sent 2026-10-23; awaiting reply.
- Docs: `../firmware/`.

## Interconnect
- Press connector (Amphenol MEG-Array, ~5.5–6 mm; backup Samtec SEARAY 0.8) under study to replace MCIO. SI: Gen4 OK without retimer; Gen5 needs retimer. Docs: `research/`.

## Power (PENDING decisions)
- Stock AC inlet (100–250 V, 6 A), custom 12 V system for 600 W target.
- Proposal: `../power/docs/power_architecture_proposal.md` — sustained caps ~105 W PPT CPU, 160 W GPU, 100 W USB PD (~500 W). Candidate module Vox EIRE600-12 (airflow and availability unresolved). Not adopted.

## Thermal
- Core test plan: `am5/thermal-core-test-plan.md`. Test runs first; CPU/GPU caps set from results.
